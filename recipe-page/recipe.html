Hot Choco Recipe

Hot chocolate is a warm and comforting drink made with milk, cocoa powder, and sugar. It is rich, creamy, and perfect for cold days or relaxing moments. This simple recipe is quick to make and requires only a few basic ingredients. Hot chocolate is loved by people of all ages and can be enjoyed anytime for a sweet and cozy experience.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 tablesppon chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk in a pan on medium flame.
  2. Add cocoa powder and sugar.
  3. Stir well until everything mixes smoothly.
  4. Add chocolate chips for extra taste (optional).
  5. Pour into a cup and serve hot.
